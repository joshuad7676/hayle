## Long description (top.gg / listing style)

Hayle — Helpful Assistant, Your Loyal Expert.

Tired of running six bots to do one bot's job? Hayle bundles the best of Carl-bot, MEE6,
Dyno, Double Counter and ServerStats into a single, loyal assistant that lives in your server.

**Highlights**

- 🎮 **Levels & XP (MEE6-style)** — chat to earn XP, climb a leaderboard, get level-up shoutouts
- 🎭 **Self-roles (Carl-bot-style)** — members grab roles with one button click
- 🛡️ **Automod (Dyno-style)** — invite-link blocking, mention-spam and flood filters
- 🔍 **Alt detection (Double Counter-style)** — verification with account-age, join-cluster and device checks
- 📊 **Server counters (ServerStats-style)** — voice channels for members, boosts, roles and more
- 🎉 **Giveaways, starboard, tags, AFK, events** — the community features you actually use
- 🎫 **Tickets with custom Q&A** + full moderation suite (kick/ban/timeout, raid protect, logs)
- 🤖 **AI assistant** — `/ask` anything, or `/generate` images (bring your own OpenAI key)
- 💬 **Works in DMs and group chats** after user-install

Free, open source, and actually configured for humans — not a spreadsheet of switches.
